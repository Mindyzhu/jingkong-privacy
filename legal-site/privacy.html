Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Lighterly handles information when you use the iPhone app or contact support. Lighterly offers tools for journaling, shopping decisions, relationships, and personal reflection. You can use the app without creating a Lighterly account.

Information We Process

Lighterly saves the information you choose to enter, such as mood ratings and journal notes, wishlist items and shopping decisions, relationship notes and check-ins, and 7-day quest goals, choices, progress, and notes. It also saves preferences, rules, and any nickname or profile photo you add.

If you use Voice input, iOS asks for microphone and speech recognition access. Lighterly requires on-device dictation, produces editable note text, and saves no audio recording from dictation. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include so we can respond.

Screen Time

With your permission, Lighterly uses Apple’s Screen Time controls to display a system picker for apps or categories you choose. Apple’s selection tokens stay in native device storage. Lighterly uses them to display saved selections and associate them with your rules. The current implementation neither reads activity totals nor enforces app limits. Screen Time data is excluded from AI requests.

Health Data

Apple Health. With your permission, Lighterly reads sleep, steps, heart rate, and resting heart rate through HealthKit. These readings may come from your iPhone, Apple Watch, or another app or device that writes to Apple Health. Lighterly requests read access only.

Direct ring sync. If you pair a compatible JY Ring, Lighterly can read sleep, steps, and heart-rate readings over Bluetooth. It remembers the ring’s device identifier and name locally and caches synced readings on the device. Direct ring readings are a separate source from Apple Health. Lighterly can use both sources to display your trends.

Lighterly processes these readings on your device for personal wellness views. The AI requests described below do not include HealthKit or ring readings. Health views are for personal wellness and do not provide medical diagnosis.

AI Features

When you request an AI feature, Lighterly sends the information needed for the requested reflection or summary over HTTPS. The app sends requests to a Cloudflare Worker. The service design uses APiBox as the default gateway to the configured AI model.

For reflections or plans, the app may send a selected mood or wishlist entry, eligible mood, wishlist, and relationship records, and context such as record identifiers, dates, the selected period, and language. Sample records and locked notes are excluded from reflection requests. When you request an AI quest summary, the app may send the quest theme, goal, target, progress, daily choices and notes, and results. The service uses the supplied information to generate your requested response, which the app can cache locally.

The reflection flow asks for optional AI sharing permission. You can turn off AI Sharing in Me for reflections. Avoid requesting an AI quest summary if you do not want your quest information sent. For questions about information already sent, email samll-pig@163.com.

Data Storage & Security

The app stores personal records, preferences, and cached AI results locally in the app installation. Lighterly does not offer an account sync service. AI requests are processed by remote services as described above. Apple manages App Store transactions, and your email provider and ours handle any support emails you send. Keep access to your iPhone and email account secure.

Third-Party Services

Apple provides the iOS permission controls, Apple Health, and App Store billing. Cloudflare hosts the configured AI request endpoint. The Worker code defaults to the APiBox gateway, which routes requests to a configured model. Email providers handle delivery when you contact us.

Data Retention & Deletion

In Me, select Clear local data and confirm to remove saved app records, preferences, cached AI replies, and saved Screen Time selections from this installation. This action does not clear the separate ring cache, delete readings from Apple Health or the ring, revoke system permissions, cancel a subscription, or delete information already sent to an AI service or by email.

To remove the app’s remaining local data from your iPhone, use Delete App. Manage Apple Health readings and permissions in the Health app. Contact us for retention or deletion questions about AI requests and support correspondence.

Subscriptions

Apple processes Lighterly Plus purchases and renewals through your Apple Account. Lighterly checks verified StoreKit transactions to determine whether Plus is active and restore access. The app does not receive your card number or other payment credentials. Manage billing and cancellation in your Apple Account subscriptions.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict its use. To make a request, email samll-pig@163.com. You can also edit or clear local records in the app and manage individual permissions through iOS or Apple Health.

Children’s Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information through an AI request or support email, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.
