Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Lighterly handles information when you use the iPhone app or contact support. Lighterly is published by Shenzhen Lesheng Technology Media Co., Ltd. The app offers tools for journaling, shopping decisions, relationships, and personal reflection. You can use the app without creating a Lighterly account.

It also covers the optional features that send information off your device: AI analysis, the Lighter Ring inquiry form, and Demo Mode. Each of those is described in its own section below.

Information We Process

Lighterly saves the information you choose to enter, such as mood ratings and journal notes, wishlist items and shopping decisions, relationship notes and check-ins, and 7-day quest goals, choices, progress, and notes. It also saves preferences, rules, and any nickname or profile photo you add.

If you use Voice input, iOS asks for microphone and speech recognition access. Lighterly requires on-device dictation, produces editable note text, and saves no audio recording from dictation. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include so we can respond.

If you use Ask about Lighter Ring, the form asks for an email address and a message. The app sends both to our endpoint so we can reply by email. We use that address only to answer the inquiry you submitted. The form is optional, and nothing is sent until you submit it.

How We Use Information

The app uses the information you enter to run the features you ask for: showing your records, building the weekly and monthly views, tracking a 7-day quest, and generating an AI response when you request one. These views are computed on your device.

We use an inquiry email address only to reply to that inquiry. We use StoreKit transaction information only to determine whether Lighterly Plus is active. We apply rate limits to incoming requests so the endpoints stay available and abuse is contained.

Apart from the AI request and the optional inquiry form described in this policy, nothing in the app sends your records anywhere. There is no account, no sync service, no advertising, and the app contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDK.

Screen Time

With your permission, Lighterly uses Apple’s Screen Time controls to display a system picker for apps or categories you choose. Apple’s selection tokens stay in native device storage. Lighterly uses them to display saved selections and associate them with your rules. The current implementation neither reads activity totals nor enforces app limits. Screen Time data is excluded from AI requests.

Health Data

Apple Health. With your permission, Lighterly reads sleep, steps, heart rate, and resting heart rate through HealthKit. These readings may come from your iPhone, Apple Watch, or another app or device that writes to Apple Health. Lighterly requests read access only.

Direct ring sync. If you pair a compatible JY Ring, Lighterly can read sleep, steps, and heart-rate readings over Bluetooth. It remembers the ring’s device identifier and name locally and caches synced readings on the device. Direct ring readings are a separate source from Apple Health. Lighterly can use both sources to display your trends.

Lighterly processes these readings on your device for personal wellness views. The AI requests described below do not include HealthKit or ring readings. Health views are for personal wellness and do not provide medical diagnosis.

AI Features

When you request an AI feature, Lighterly sends the information needed for that request over HTTPS to our endpoint, which runs on Cloudflare. A request is only sent after you allow AI Sharing, and only when you ask for an analysis or a summary.

Reflections on a mood or wishlist entry. The request contains record identifiers, the dates and period you selected, your app language, and the selected entry: the mood rating and its note text, or the wishlist title, note, price, and status. It also contains the other eligible records in that period.

Weekly and monthly reflections. The request contains the same record types for the selected period, plus aggregate counts for that period and the period before it, along with record identifiers and dates used to describe patterns. It does not contain Screen Time activity data.

7-day quest summaries. The request contains the quest theme, goal, target, progress, the daily choices and notes you entered, and the results.

The app does not send your nickname, your profile photo, Apple Health readings, ring readings, or Screen Time data. Built-in example entries and time-locked notes are excluded from reflection and summary requests.

Replies come back as structured text. The app checks that any record the reply cites actually exists, then saves the result on your device so you can reopen it without sending another request.

In Demo Mode the sample records shown on screen are exactly what a request contains. They are generated on your device and contain no personal data.

AI Service Providers

A request travels through the following services, in this order. Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the endpoint that receives it. APiBox is the gateway that the endpoint uses by default to reach the model. The model configured for Lighterly is a Claude model from Anthropic, identified as claude-sonnet-4-6.

Cloudflare and Anthropic each publish a privacy policy: Cloudflare and Anthropic. Those documents describe how each company handles data for its own services. We have not been able to verify a published privacy policy for APiBox, so this page links to none and summarises none.

Our endpoint writes no database of request content. Its logs record a request identifier, timing, character counts, HTTP status, and the model name. When a provider returns an error, the log also keeps a truncated, redacted copy of that provider error message. The reply text is not logged, and responses are not stored by the endpoint.

What we can confirm. Anthropic states that, for Anthropic API traffic, it automatically deletes inputs and outputs from its backend within 30 days, and that it does not use those inputs or outputs to train its models unless the customer explicitly opts in. Anthropic also states that it may keep data longer where it is enforcing its usage policy (up to two years for inputs and outputs), where the law requires it, or where a customer agreement provides otherwise.

What we cannot yet confirm. Our requests reach Anthropic through the APiBox account rather than an account we hold directly, so we cannot confirm that those terms apply to this traffic in the same way. We have also not verified an operating entity or a published privacy policy for APiBox, nor where the request is processed. We therefore state no retention period of our own for AI requests. If you need that detail before sharing sensitive text, email us first, or keep sensitive details out of the notes you analyze.

Your AI Sharing Choice

AI Sharing is off until you allow it. The first time you open an analysis, the app explains what will be sent and asks you to allow or decline. Nothing is sent before you allow it.

You can turn AI Sharing off at any time in Me. Turning it off while a request is in flight cancels that request. If you decline or turn it off, the rest of the app keeps working: your records, views, quests, and reports stay available, and only the AI actions stop.

Quest summaries use the same switch. The app asks for the same permission before it sends one.

The app stores locally which version of this disclosure you agreed to and when, so a later change to this policy can be recognized. Turning AI Sharing off does not recall a request that already reached the service, and it does not delete a reply already cached on your device. Clear local data removes cached replies.

Demo Mode

Demo Mode lets you explore Lighterly with sample data, including on a device with no ring. You can open it from Me → Demo Mode, or from the sample-data link on a health screen. Both lead to the same place.

While Demo Mode is active, the app generates sample records and 30 days of sample health readings on your device. They are stored separately from your real data. Your records, preferences, Apple Health data, ring readings, and Screen Time selections are not read, changed, or deleted. Demo Mode does not request HealthKit, Bluetooth, or Screen Time permission, and the device screen shows no ring connected.

Demo data is fictional. If you request AI analysis while in Demo Mode, those sample records are the content of the request, under the same AI Sharing rule described above. Nothing personal is included.

Plus features are available inside Demo Mode so the full experience can be explored without a purchase. That is not a subscription, it does not create or change a StoreKit transaction, and it ends with Demo Mode.

Exiting Demo Mode deletes the sample records and the demo preferences, and returns you to your own data and your verified Plus status.

Data Storage & Security

The app stores personal records, preferences, and cached AI results locally in the app installation. Lighterly does not offer an account sync service. AI requests and ring inquiries are sent over HTTPS to the endpoints described above and are processed by those remote services. Apple manages App Store transactions, and your email provider and ours handle any support emails you send. Keep access to your iPhone and email account secure.

Third-Party Services

Apple provides the iOS permission controls, Apple Health, Screen Time controls, and App Store billing. Cloudflare hosts the AI endpoint and the ring inquiry endpoint. APiBox is the AI gateway that routes requests to the configured model, which is a Claude model from Anthropic. Resend delivers the email we send when you submit a ring inquiry. Email providers handle delivery when you contact us.

Privacy policies for these companies: Cloudflare, Anthropic, Resend, and Apple.

Data Retention & Deletion

In Me, select Clear local data and confirm to remove saved app records, preferences, cached AI replies, and saved Screen Time selections from this installation. This action does not clear the separate ring cache, delete readings from Apple Health or the ring, revoke system permissions, cancel a subscription, or delete information already sent to an AI service or by email.

To remove the app’s remaining local data from your iPhone, use Delete App. Manage Apple Health readings and permissions in the Health app. Contact us for retention or deletion questions about AI requests and support correspondence.

Our AI endpoint keeps no database of request content, as described under AI Service Providers. Ring inquiry emails stay in our mailbox so we can answer them; email us if you want a specific inquiry deleted. Requests already delivered to a third-party provider are subject to that provider’s own retention, as described under AI Service Providers.

Subscriptions

Apple processes Lighterly Plus purchases and renewals through your Apple Account. Lighterly checks verified StoreKit transactions to determine whether Plus is active and restore access. The app does not receive your card number or other payment credentials. Manage billing and cancellation in your Apple Account subscriptions.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict its use. To make a request, email samll-pig@163.com. You can also edit or clear local records in the app and manage individual permissions through iOS or Apple Health.

Children’s Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information through an AI request or support email, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.
