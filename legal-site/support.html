Getting Started

What is Lighterly?

Lighterly is an iPhone app for pausing, journaling, and noticing patterns in your habits and decisions. You can use the core tools without a Lighterly account.

How do I get started?

Open Today and choose the area you want to explore. Try a mood check-in, save something to your Wishlist, or start a quest. Grant permissions when you want to use features that need them.

How does the 7-day challenge work?

Choose a free 7-day quest and follow one small action per day. You can record whether you completed, attempted, or skipped an action. At the end, review your saved steps. Plus adds an optional AI summary.

Features

Screen Time

On a supported iPhone, grant Screen Time access and use Apple’s picker to choose apps or categories when setting up a rule. The current implementation saves rules for your reference. It does not block apps or display usage totals.

Mood

Log how you feel and add a note if you like. If Voice input is available for your language, you can dictate an editable note after granting microphone and speech recognition access.

Wishlist

Save something you’re considering buying and revisit the decision later. Use a purchase reflection to consider what you want and how the purchase fits your needs.

Social

Use Connections to log personal notes and reflect on your relationships.

Reflections

Open Insights to review recent patterns. Plus members can request AI reflections with an internet connection. For more information about what is shared, see the AI Features section.

Health & Devices

How do I connect a smart ring?

In My Devices, tap Find ring. Keep a charged, compatible JY Ring near your iPhone, allow Bluetooth access, and select the ring. Confirm pairing if iOS asks. For other rings that support Apple Health, use the ring maker’s app to write readings to Apple Health.

How do I enable Apple Health?

In My Devices, tap Enable Apple Health and allow Lighterly to read the categories you want to see. You can manage access in the Health app under your profile and the list of apps with Health access.

Why isn’t my health data showing?

Check that the readings are available for the dates you’re viewing. For Apple Health, check the readings in Health and confirm Lighterly’s read permissions. For direct ring sync, keep the ring nearby and charged, check Bluetooth, and make sure the ring is connected.

How do I sync or disconnect a device?

Keep the ring nearby and connected to allow it to sync. You can also use the sync control on the health report. To remove a paired ring from Lighterly, open My Devices, choose Remove ring, and confirm. Previously synced ring data may remain on the phone. To disconnect Apple Health, manage Lighterly’s read access in the Health app.

Subscription & Billing

What’s included in Lighterly Plus?

Plus includes AI reflections and personalized AI quest summaries. The Plus screen shows available subscriptions and App Store prices for your region.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Me, select Explore Lighterly Plus, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account you originally subscribed with.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, select Subscriptions, and choose Lighterly Plus. Deleting Lighterly does not cancel your subscription. See Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Privacy & Data

How is my data used?

Lighterly keeps core records on your device and uses remote processing for requested AI features. Read the Privacy Policy for details on the information used and your choices.

How can I delete my data?

Open Me, tap Clear local data, and confirm. To remove the app’s remaining local data, use Delete App in iOS. See Data Retention & Deletion for what each option covers.

How do I change permissions or stop sharing?

Use AI Sharing in Me to turn off sharing for AI reflections. Skip the AI quest summary if you want to keep quest details on your device. Manage Apple Health access in Health and Screen Time, Bluetooth, microphone, speech recognition, and notifications through iOS settings.
