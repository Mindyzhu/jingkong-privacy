Common questions

Why can’t some folders be scanned?

macOS permissions and system protections can restrict access. Check authorized folders in settings and select a folder again if access has expired. An unmeasured folder is not necessarily empty.

Why is free space unchanged after moving files to Trash?

Moving files to Trash generally does not immediately free space. Review the files before emptying Trash through Finder. Once emptied, files generally cannot be restored from Trash.

How can I restore a file moved to Trash?

If Trash has not been emptied, locate the file in Finder’s Trash and choose Put Back if available, or move it to an appropriate location. If Trash has been emptied, check your backups.

What if a purchase did not unlock the app?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and select Restore purchases in MacSense settings. Check your connection and try again. If the issue persists, send the app version and an error screenshot. Never send your Apple Account password.

How do I manage a subscription?

Use Apple’s subscription management page. Uninstalling MacSense does not cancel a subscription. Available products, prices and entitlements are those displayed in the app and App Store.

How do I stop folder access or cleanup rules?

Remove the folder authorization in settings and disable any cleanup rules you no longer want. Removing access does not automatically erase existing local records. Contact us for help locating or deleting them.
