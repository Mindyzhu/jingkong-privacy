Privacy policy

A clear account of what MonoSense processes and where it stays.

01 · Local files and scanning

MonoSense analyzes files and folders it can access on your Mac. It processes names, paths, file types, sizes, timestamps and disk capacity to show storage usage and cleanup suggestions. Some identification features read application metadata such as Info.plist to identify an application and its associated files. Scanning and identification run locally. MonoSense does not automatically upload scanned folders, file contents or scan results to the developer.

02 · Information stored on your Mac

MonoSense stores folder authorization bookmarks, scan summaries (including root paths and storage statistics), cleanup rules, review items, run records, an application index and icon caches locally. Rules and records may include file or folder paths, times, sizes, outcomes and error details. Some errors may also be written to local system logs.

The trial start time and related state are stored in the local Keychain and/or application storage. A purchase entitlement cache includes product and transaction identifiers, verification times and expiration information. MonoSense does not automatically upload these records to developer servers.

03 · Purchases and Apple services

MonoSense uses Apple StoreKit to load products, make and restore purchases, and check entitlements. Apple processes these transactions, which may require a connection to Apple services. MonoSense reads transaction and entitlement information returned by Apple; it does not receive your bank card number or full payment details. Apple handles information within its services under its own privacy policy.

Read Apple’s privacy policy

04 · Permissions and your choices

Access is granted through macOS file selection and authorization mechanisms. You can remove authorized folders in MonoSense settings. Removing access stops subsequent access under that authorization; it does not automatically erase existing summaries, rules or records. System protections and permissions may prevent access to some folders.

Cleanup is started by you or runs within the rules and permissions you enable. Files moved to Trash generally still use disk space. Review them before deciding whether to empty Trash.

05 · Sharing, tracking and support

MonoSense currently contains no advertising tracking or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs and does not collect scan data through a developer-operated server. No MonoSense account registration is required.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer questions, investigate issues and follow up. Email is processed by the respective email service providers. Do not send passwords, sensitive payment information or unrelated personal files. Redact personal details in screenshots and paths first.

06 · Retention, deletion and contact

Local records are retained for their respective functions; some histories have entry limits. There is no single automatic expiration period for all local data. Uninstalling the app may leave trial records in Keychain and does not cancel Apple subscriptions.

Contact the privacy email for help locating or deleting local records, or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you supplied by support email. We will verify information needed to handle your request and explain the outcome, including any information we need to retain and why.

07 · This website and policy changes

This website includes no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or browser local storage. Once publicly hosted, the hosting provider may process request information such as IP addresses and access times to operate and secure its service, under its own policies.

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change, with notice in the app or on this page where needed.
